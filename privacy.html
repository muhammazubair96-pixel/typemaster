Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TypeMaster. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the TypeMaster website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

TypeMaster is designed to provide typing practice with minimal data collection. Depending on the features you use, the website may process information such as:

  • Typing test results such as WPM and accuracy.
  • Level completion and progress information.
  • Information entered into optional contact forms.
  • Custom text entered into the typing practice tool.
  • Basic technical information required for the website to function.

3. Typing Test Data

During a typing test, TypeMaster may calculate information such as typing speed, accuracy, character count, mistakes, completion progress and test time. These calculations are primarily used to provide your typing results and improve the functionality of the typing experience.

4. Local Storage

TypeMaster may use your browser's local storage to remember information such as unlocked typing levels and basic progress. Local storage data remains in your browser unless it is cleared by you or removed by the website.

5. Contact Information

If you use our contact form, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, subject and message. This information may be used to respond to your request, provide support, or address feedback.

6. Cookies

TypeMaster may use cookies or similar technologies in the future to improve website functionality, understand website usage, or provide optional services. If third-party advertising or analytics services are added, their own privacy policies may also apply.

7. Third-Party Services

Some website features may use third-party services, libraries, APIs, analytics platforms, hosting providers, or other external technologies. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. AI Services

If TypeMaster introduces AI-powered features such as spelling correction, text generation or typing content generation, text submitted to those features may be processed by an external AI service. Users should avoid submitting confidential, sensitive, personal, financial or private information into AI-powered features.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the website. However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

TypeMaster is intended to provide a general typing practice service. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe that a child has submitted personal information may contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

11. External Links

TypeMaster may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security or policies of websites operated by third parties. Users should review the privacy policy of any external website they visit.

12. Your Choices

You may stop using the website at any time. You can also clear browser storage and cookies through your browser settings. If you have submitted information through a contact form and want to ask about that information, you can contact us through the Contact page.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, features, services or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how TypeMaster handles information, please visit our Contact Us page.

Important Notice
This privacy policy is a general website template and should be reviewed and adapted to your actual data collection, hosting, analytics, advertising, payment, authentication and AI services before publishing it as your final legal privacy policy.

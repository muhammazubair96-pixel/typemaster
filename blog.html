How to Increase Your Typing Speed

The best way to increase typing speed is to practice consistently. Begin with simple words and focus on pressing the correct keys.

Avoid looking at your keyboard while typing. Instead, learn the position of the keys and allow your fingers to develop muscle memory.

Do not sacrifice accuracy just to increase WPM. A steady typing rhythm with fewer mistakes will help you become faster over time.

How to Improve Typing Accuracy

Accuracy means typing the correct characters without making unnecessary mistakes.

Start slowly and concentrate on every word. If you make frequent mistakes, reduce your speed and focus on correct finger placement.

With regular practice, your accuracy will improve and your typing speed will naturally become faster.

What Does WPM Mean?

WPM stands for Words Per Minute.

Typing systems commonly calculate one word as approximately five characters, including spaces. Your WPM shows how quickly you can produce text during a typing test.

For example, if your result is 40 WPM, you are typing at an average rate of approximately 40 standard words per minute.
